Why folks keep coming back

We treat coffee like the ingredient it is.

We roast it ourselves

Small batches on our own roaster, so the beans in your cup are days-fresh — never sitting in a warehouse for months.

Sourced with names attached

Direct-trade lots from farms we can point to on a map. We pay above market and tell you exactly whose coffee you're drinking.

Baristas who dial in

Every espresso is weighed and timed. Ask a question and we'll happily geek out with you about grind size and extraction.
